# Where the data came from (downloaded 2026-10-25)

## What I searched and didn't find
No public dataset shows eye drops filmed from a bottle/nozzle camera with "went in / missed" labels.
Checked: Google Scholar style search, Roboflow Universe, Hugging Face, Kaggle listings, Wikimedia Commons.
- Hugging Face "eye drops" datasets are all robot arms picking up bottles (LeRobot), not instillation.
- Roboflow "drop" (mikido/drop-jtzrf, CC BY 4.0) has an `Eye_Drop` box class but needs a Roboflow API key. Not downloaded.
- Closest research: Hirai/Kashiwagi-style video grading studies (drops graded by masked observers), and a 2019 TVST bottle motion-sensor + deep learning study. Neither released data.
  - https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4711577/
  - https://tvst.arvojournals.org/article.aspx?articleid=2737189

## Downloaded
| Folder | What | License | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `data/raw/periorbital/` | Nahass et al. 2024, Open-Source Periorbital Segmentation Dataset, 2,842 face crops (Chicago Face Database + CelebAMask-HQ) with iris/sclera/caruncle/lid/brow masks. https://zenodo.org/records/13916845 | CC BY 4.0 | Eye backgrounds for simulated drops. The masks decide whether a drop landed in the eye. |
| `data/raw/pexels/eye/` | 14 close-up eye videos (blinks, looking around) | Pexels license (free use) | Real-footage false-alarm test, nothing falls in them |
| `data/raw/pexels/instill/` | 5 videos of people putting in eye drops, filmed from across the room | Pexels license | Reference only. Wrong viewpoint, the drop is barely visible. |
| `data/raw/pexels/drip/` | 3 dropper close-ups | Pexels license | Reference for how a drop looks |

Pexels IDs and URLs are in `data/raw/pexels/urls.tsv`.
`data/eyes/` is generated by `prep_eyes.py` (5,617 single-eye 160x120 crops + masks).
